## SpreadFast is taking new clients

We analyze your data, find the problems, and give you solutions that grow your business.

We're a team of three. We work with small business owners who run restaurants, clothing shops and online stores.

### How we work

1. **Study your category.** We learn how your kind of business works before we look at yours.
2. **Study customers.** We look at how customers in your category tend to buy.
3. **Audit your own business.** Then we look at your own numbers, so our advice fits you.

### What you get from a first look

- **It's free.** The first look includes an audit of your own numbers.
- We tell you what we'd check first. We won't say what's wrong until we've seen your data.
- We may estimate the size of the opportunity. It's an estimate, not a promise, and we'll tell you where it comes from.
- If you'd like us to go further after the audit, we'll give you a clear price before you agree to anything.

### Your data

We only ask for what we need. Only the three of us see it. We never sell it or share it with anyone else.

### Send a first-look request

It's a short form. We aim to reply to every request within two working days.

- Your type of business (restaurant, clothing shop, online store, other)
- Your main problem, in a sentence or two
- A rough sales range
- Best time to talk (optional)
