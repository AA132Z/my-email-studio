# SpreadFast brand voice

SpreadFast is a three-person analytics team. We help US small business owners in food service (QSR), real estate, apparel and e-commerce. We find owners who have posted a problem in public, such as "sales are fine but margins keep dropping." We reply there first, and we email them only after they give us their address.

What we tell every client: **we analyze your data, find the problems, and give you solutions that grow your business.**

Our method, in the order we explain it:
1. Study your category, to see how it works.
2. Study customers in general.
3. Audit your own business, so our advice fits you.

## What we offer

Three services, each tied directly to revenue:

- **Sales analytics**: what sells, when, and at what margin. One method we use is Market Basket Analysis, which means finding which items people buy together.
- **Consumer analytics**: who your customers are and how they behave. One method we use is RFM, which means sorting customers by how recently, how often and how much they buy.
- **Communication analysis**: what your messages and ads say, and how they land with customers.

For each category we use well-known frameworks that fit how that kind of business works. Name a method once, and explain it in plain words right after.

## How we win a client

1. **Find and reply.** We find an owner on Reddit or a similar site who has posted a business problem. We reply there, offer help, and ask for their email. This step is not email.
2. **Category review.** Once they share their email, we send a custom review of their category (attached) and a few custom questions.
3. **Data agreed.** Once they agree to share their data, we say what to send, how we keep it private, and when our proposal will come.
4. **Proposal.** After we've studied their data, we send a custom proposal with our quote (attached). We only quote after we've seen the data.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Friendly.** We write like a helpful person, not a firm. If we sound stiff or salesy, worried owners leave.
- **Careful.** We say what we would look at first, not what we will fix. We ask before we assume.
- **Clear.** One plain point and one clear number, with more detail for anyone who wants it. Short sentences and everyday words.

## 2. Words we use

- your own data, your numbers, your sales, your margins
- your category, your customers, your business
- what fits your business, what's relevant to you
- we'd start by looking at…, here's what we'd check first
- opportunity, the size of the opportunity (always as an estimate)
- we estimate, our analysis suggests, from what you've shared
- advanced analytics and AI, but only once, and always explained in plain words right after it (for example: "we use advanced analytics, which means we look for patterns in your sales and costs")

## 3. Words we avoid

- Hype: revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, explode, skyrocket, 10x
- Jargon: leverage, synergy, actionable insights, data-driven solutions, holistic, end-to-end, optimize (say "improve"), KPIs (say "the numbers that matter")
- "AI-powered" or "advanced analytics" with no explanation
- Pressure: last chance, act now, limited time, don't miss out, only a few spots left, urgent
- Fear: you're losing money every day, your business is failing
- Overfamiliar or fake: "Hey friend", "Quick question" subject lines, "Re:" on a first email

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No guaranteed results.** Never "we'll double your sales," "we'll fix your margins," or "guaranteed growth."
- **No promised percentages.** Never "you'll grow 30%" or "cut costs by 20%."
- **Opportunity size is allowed, but only as an estimate.** Say where it comes from and that it is not a promise. Allowed: "From our category study, we estimate about $4,000 a month in margin is at stake." Not allowed: "You will gain $4,000 a month."
- **No client names or results** without that client's written permission.
- **Don't make us sound bigger than we are.** We are a small team of three. No "our team of experts," "hundreds of clients," or "industry-leading."
- **Don't diagnose before we've seen the data.** Before the audit, we say what we'd check, not what's wrong.
- **Stay inside what we do.** We analyze business data. We never make health or wellness claims (such as "detox" or "boosts your energy"), and we never give legal, tax or investment advice. If an owner asks, we suggest they speak to the right professional.

## 5. How we sign off

Every email ends like this:

> Happy to look at your numbers,
> Team SpreadFast

One of us sends the emails, but always signs as "Team SpreadFast." Personal names, phone numbers and email addresses don't go in the sign-off.
