# SpreadFast launch plan

Business: SpreadFast, a three-person team that provides advanced analytics for business growth (we look for patterns in a business's sales, costs and customers, then suggest what to change)
Channel: email only, no paid ads
Campaign window: Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
Voice: follows `brand/voice.md`. Friendly, careful and clear. Every email ends "Happy to look at your numbers, Team SpreadFast".

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** SpreadFast Is Open
- **Summary:** Tell about 2,000 small business owners on our list that SpreadFast is taking new clients, and invite each one to send a free "first look" request.
- **Goal:** 300 owners become prospective clients by Sunday 3 January 2027. A prospective client is an owner who sends us a first-look request: a short form with their business type, their main problem and a rough sales range.
- **Other aims:**
  - Learn which of our three groups sends the most requests, so we know where to focus next.
  - Keep unsubscribes low (under 2% across the four emails).

300 out of about 2,000 is about 15 in every 100. That is a high bar for email alone, so we track it every week (see section 7).

## 2. Who we are writing to

About 2,000 US small business owners asked to hear from us. This campaign goes to three groups: apparel, QSR and e-commerce owners. Real estate owners are not part of this campaign (decided 15 November 2026); we'll write to them in a later campaign. Recount the list without them before 7 December, and check that the goal of 300 still makes sense. Most posted a business problem in public, such as "sales are fine but margins keep dropping." We replied there first, and they then shared their email with us. They know us a little, so they are warm, not cold.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might stop them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apparel owners | Sell clothes in a shop, online or both. | To know which products, sizes and seasons really make money, and why stock piles up. | Worry about sharing sales data. Think analytics is only for big brands. |
| QSR owners | Run one to a few quick-service restaurants. Busy every day. | To know why margins are slipping while sales hold up, and what to change first. | No time. Doubt that a small team can help a business their size. |
| E-commerce owners | Sell online and check their numbers every day. | To know which customers, products and channels bring profit, not just sales. | Already have dashboards and tools. Unsure what we add. |

All three groups have a problem they want solved, but they don't know us well yet. The emails build trust first, then ask for a first-look request.

## 3. Key messages

**Main message:** We analyze your data, find the problems, and give you solutions that grow your business.

Supporting messages:
1. **We start with your category.** We study how your kind of business works before we look at yours, so our advice fits.
2. **Then your customers, then your business.** We look at customers in general, then audit your own numbers. Nothing generic.
3. **We show you the size of the opportunity.** We give an estimate of what is at stake, and say where it comes from. It is an estimate, not a promise.
4. **The first look is free and careful.** It includes an audit of your own numbers. We tell you what we would check first. We don't say what's wrong until we've seen your data.

Messages by group (only the opening line and one example change):
- **Apparel:** "Is stock you can't sell eating your profit?"
- **QSR:** "Sales are steady, but are your margins slipping?"
- **E-commerce:** "You see your sales every day. Do you see which ones make money?"

Proof we can use: our three-step method and a made-up sample category study, clearly marked as a sample. We don't name clients or share client results without written permission. We never promise a set result or a percentage, and we don't make ourselves sound bigger than three people.

## 7. How we'll measure success

**Main number:** first-look requests sent by owners, with a target of 300 by Sunday 3 January 2027.

Weekly check-points (rough targets, so we can spot trouble early):

| End of week | Requests so far |
|---|---|
| Sunday 13 December 2026 | 120 |
| Sunday 20 December 2026 | 200 |
| Sunday 27 December 2026 | 250 |
| Sunday 3 January 2027 | 300 |

Other numbers we watch:
- Opens and clicks for each email, by group
- Requests by group (apparel, QSR, e-commerce)
- Replies to our emails (owners often reply instead of clicking)
- Requests that turn into a first-look call
- Unsubscribes for each email (aim: under 2% in total)

How we track them: the email tool's reports for opens, clicks and unsubscribes, plus a tracking tag on the form link so each request shows which email and group it came from. We log replies by hand. We review the numbers every Monday.

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| 300 requests is a lot for a team of three to answer. | Reply to every request within two working days with a short note. Book calls first with owners whose problem fits our three steps best. Tell owners plainly when we can start. |
| December is the busiest month for restaurants and shops, so owners may not have time to read. | Keep emails short. Email 4 offers a January start. If numbers are well under the weekly check-point, move the next campaign to Monday 4 January 2027. |
| Owners worry about sharing their sales data. | Explain in email 3 what data we need and how we keep it private. Only say what is true, and confirm it before email 3 goes out. |
| We sound like every other "AI" company. | Explain our three steps in plain words. Say "advanced analytics" once at most, and explain it right after. Never promise results or percentages. |

Decisions made (15 November 2026):

- Data privacy: we only ask for what we need, only the three of us see it, and we never sell or share it.
- Price: we don't name a starting price. After the free first look and audit, we give a clear price before the owner agrees to anything.
- Reply time: we aim to reply to every request within two working days.

- Real estate owners are not part of this campaign.
