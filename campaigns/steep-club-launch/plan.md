# The Steep Club launch plan

Client: Kettlebird Tea (made-up practice client)
Channel: email only, no paid ads
Campaign window: Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Your First Steep
- **Summary:** Invite our waitlist to join The Steep Club, a monthly tea box, with four clear, friendly emails over four weeks.
- **Goal:** 300 waitlist members start a subscription by Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **Other aims:**
  - Learn which of our three groups signs up most, so we know who to write to next.
  - Keep unsubscribes low (under 2% across the four emails).

The goal means about 15 in every 100 waitlist members sign up. That is a high bar for email alone, so we track it every week (see section 7).

## 2. Who we are writing to

About 2,000 people who joined the Kettlebird Tea waitlist. They already asked to hear from us, so they are warm, not cold.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might stop them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tea-curious beginners | New to loose-leaf tea. Like the idea, unsure where to start. | Simple steps, a gentle start, no tea snobbery. | Fear of getting it wrong, or paying for tea they won't like. |
| Gift buyers | Want a present for a tea lover, with Christmas close. | A gift that feels personal and arrives on time. | Not knowing the delivery dates, or how a gift box works. |
| Daily tea drinkers | Drink tea every day and know what they like. | New teas worth trying, good value, freedom to skip. | Being locked into a plan, or getting teas they already have. |

All three groups are near the point of deciding. They signed up to hear about the box, so the emails can be direct about what it is and how to join.

## 3. Key messages

**Main message:** A new tea to try each month, chosen for you and delivered to your door.

Supporting messages:
1. **It's easy to start.** Each box comes with a short card on how to brew each tea. (Best for beginners.)
2. **It makes a thoughtful gift.** You can send a box to someone else, with a note from you. (Best for gift buyers.)
3. **You stay in charge.** Skip a month or cancel any time, in two clicks. (Best for daily drinkers.)
4. **Waitlist members heard first.** You asked to hear about it, so you are the first to know.

Proof we can use: what is in the first box, the brewing card and the skip-or-cancel steps. We have no customer reviews yet, so we don't quote any. We make no health claims about any tea.

## 4. Channel plan

| Channel | Why it fits | What we send | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email | The whole audience is on our waitlist. It costs little and goes straight to people who asked to hear from us. | 4 emails, each with one version per group | Low to medium |

No paid ads, social posts or PR in this plan. A sign-up page for The Steep Club must be live before the first email goes out.

## 7. How we'll measure success

**Main number:** subscriptions started by waitlist members, with a target of 300 by Sunday 3 January 2027.

Weekly check-points (rough targets, so we can spot trouble early):

| End of week | Sign-ups so far |
|---|---|
| Sunday 13 December 2026 | 120 |
| Sunday 20 December 2026 | 200 |
| Sunday 27 December 2026 | 250 |
| Sunday 3 January 2027 | 300 |

Other numbers we watch:
- Opens and clicks for each email, by group
- Sign-ups by group (beginners, gift buyers, daily drinkers)
- Gift boxes as a share of all sign-ups
- Unsubscribes for each email (aim: under 2% in total)

How we track them: the email tool's reports for opens, clicks and unsubscribes, plus a tracking tag on the sign-up link so each sign-up shows which email and group it came from. We review the numbers every Monday.

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| 300 sign-ups is a lot for email alone. | Check numbers every Monday. If we are well under the week's check-point, change email 3 or 4 to speak to the group that is signing up least. |
| Gift buyers miss Christmas delivery. | Confirm the Christmas delivery date with the warehouse before email 2, and state it clearly. Don't promise a date we can't keep. |
| Holiday inboxes are full, so fewer people open. | Keep subject lines short and plain. Email 4 goes only to people who haven't joined, so we don't over-mail new members. |

## 10. Next steps

1. Build and test the sign-up page, including the gift option.
2. Confirm price, what's in the first box, and the Christmas delivery date.
3. Write the four emails and save them in `campaigns/steep-club-launch/emails/`.
4. Write one version of each email per group in `campaigns/steep-club-launch/groups/`.

Decision points:
- Do we offer anything extra to people who join in the first month? If yes, it must be a plain, true offer, with no pressure wording.
- Is a gift box a one-off, or a subscription the buyer pays for each month?
