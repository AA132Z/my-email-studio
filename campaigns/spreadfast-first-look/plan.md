# SpreadFast "First Look" campaign plan

Business: SpreadFast, a three-person team that provides advanced analytics for business growth (we look for patterns in a business's sales, costs and customers, then suggest what to change)
Channel: email only, no paid ads
Campaign window: Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** First Look
- **Summary:** Follow up with small business owners who shared their email after we replied to their post, and invite them to a free 30-minute "first look" call about their numbers.
- **Goal:** 20 owners book a free first-look call by Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **Other aims:**
  - Learn which of our three groups books most, so we know where to spend our time next.
  - Keep unsubscribes low (under 3% across the four emails).

About 150 owners are on the list, so the goal means about 13 in every 100 book a call. That is a fair bar for people who already asked us for help, but we track it every week (see section 7).

## 2. Who we are writing to

About 150 US small business owners. Each one posted a business problem in public, such as "sales are fine but margins keep dropping." We replied there first, and they then shared their email with us. They already know us a little, so they are warm, not cold.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might stop them |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurant owners (QSR) | Run one to a few quick-service outlets. Busy every day. | To know why margins are slipping, and what to change first. | No time. Doubt that "analytics" is for a business their size. |
| Shop owners (apparel and e-commerce) | Sell clothes or goods online, in a shop, or both. | To know which products, customers and channels really make money. | Worry about sharing sales data. Have tried tools that didn't help. |
| Real estate owners | Run a small agency or manage a few properties. | To know where leads come from and which ones turn into deals. | Data spread across many places. Unsure what we would need from them. |

All three groups have a problem they want solved, but they don't know us well yet. The emails need to build trust before they ask for a call.

## 3. Key messages

**Main message:** We analyze your data, find the problems, and give you solutions that grow your business.

Supporting messages:
1. **We start with your category.** We study how your kind of business works before we look at yours, so our advice fits.
2. **Then your customers, then your business.** We look at customers in general, then audit your own numbers. Nothing generic.
3. **We show you the size of the opportunity.** We give an estimate of what is at stake, and say where the estimate comes from. We never promise a result.
4. **The first look is free and careful.** On a 30-minute call, we tell you what we would check first. We don't diagnose before we see the data.

Proof we can use: our three-step method and a made-up sample of a category study. We don't name clients or share client results without written permission. We never promise a set result or a percentage.

## 4. Channel plan

## 7. How we'll measure success

**Main number:** first-look calls booked, with a target of 20 by Sunday 3 January 2027.

Weekly check-points (rough targets, so we can spot trouble early):

| End of week | Calls booked so far |
|---|---|
| Sunday 13 December 2026 | 6 |
| Sunday 20 December 2026 | 11 |
| Sunday 27 December 2026 | 15 |
| Sunday 3 January 2027 | 20 |

Other numbers we watch:
- Opens and clicks for each email, by group
- Calls booked by group (restaurants, shops, real estate)
- Replies to our emails (owners often reply instead of clicking)
- Unsubscribes for each email (aim: under 3% in total)

How we track them: the email tool's reports for opens, clicks and unsubscribes, plus a tracking tag on the booking link so each call shows which email and group it came from. Sohail logs replies by hand. We review the numbers every Monday.

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| December is the busiest month for restaurants and shops, so owners may not have time to read. | Keep emails short. Email 4 offers January calls, after the busy season. If numbers are low, move the campaign start to Monday 4 January 2027. |
| Owners worry about sharing their sales data. | Explain in email 3 what data we need and how we keep it private. Only say what is true, and confirm it before email 3 goes out. |
| We sound like every other "AI" company. | Explain our three steps in plain words. Say "advanced analytics" once at most, and explain it right after. Never promise results or percentages. |
